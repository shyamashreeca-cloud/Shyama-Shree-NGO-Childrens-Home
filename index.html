ABOUT SHYAMA SHREE

Building a Childhood Filled With Care & Hope

Shyama Shree NGO & Children's Home is dedicated to supporting children who need a safe, caring and nurturing environment.

Our approach focuses on more than basic shelter. We aim to support children's education, nutrition, health, emotional wellbeing, confidence and life skills so that they can move towards a positive and independent future.

We believe that every child deserves dignity, respect, encouragement and opportunities to discover their abilities.
